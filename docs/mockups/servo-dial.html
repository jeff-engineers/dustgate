Setting home on a bus servo

The servo is drawn as you see it standing at the gate: from behind, its back toward you, with the flag on the far side showing through (X-ray). The user sets one position, home, by eye. Every other position comes from the gate's design: a manifold turns 71.7° either way from home, and a valve 45° either way. Counter-clockwise means as you see it from behind. Flag and body are to scale from the CAD.

Questions for you

  1. Does the servo hold at the centre while the flag goes on? Here it holds, so pushing the coupler onto the spline can't turn the shaft. The other choice is to let it go limp and turn it back to centre after.
  2. The valve's servo is drawn tilted 45° clockwise, with the duct running up and down behind it, so home is half open, open is straight up (45° counter-clockwise) and closed is across (45° clockwise). Is that how the valve sits?
  3. Manifold home is closed, the flag pointing up between the two legs. Is the left tool the one on your left as you stand behind the servo?
  4. The check step lets you try each position before Done. Worth keeping, or straight to Done?
  5. Fine-tune steps are 1° and 5°. The flag goes on the spline in steps of roughly 14°, so the most it ever needs is about 7° either way.
