Plugs live on the tool, in a shop with more than one collector

Two things at once, because they meet on the same canvas. Every machine is drawn once and carries its own smart plug in the row under its name — never on a port, because a plug is one fact about a machine however many ducts reach it. And the shop has two collectors: pick a system to work on it, or keep the whole shop in view. Drag a plug out of the tray onto a machine, or let the names match themselves.

One machine, one plug, two systems

The Table Saw has two ports: its main outlet on the Main system and an overarm pickup on the Back wall. It is drawn once, with two dots under the name — one per port, each lighting when that duct is carrying air.

Its plug doesn't belong to either port. Switch the saw on and both ports want air; which of them actually gets it is a routing answer, not an electrical one.

Focus a system and the plumbing dims. Machines never do — the saw is fully lit in both, because it is genuinely in both.

Matching by name

A Shelly arrives called whatever someone typed into the Shelly app. Matching that against the machine names on this canvas saves pairing every plug by hand, walking to each machine to switch it on.

It is always a suggestion: the row shows the plug's name and waits for the tick. Being wrong costs a glance.
