DustGate carrier wiring

The one PWM board — gate, CT-sensing node or collector by what's fitted — drawn twice: the perfboard as it runs in the shop today with the new CT network added, and the PCB for the next prototype round with the sourced parts. Pad numbers come from firmware/boards/xiao_c5.h; the reasoning lives in firmware/WIRING.md, the part numbers in docs/BOM.md.

Before soldering — check these on the bench

The jack and the clamp

  • Nothing inserted: tip contact (Left / pin 2) to the switch (LSw / pin 10) beeps; insert a plug and it opens.
  • SCT-013-030 plug: tip to sleeve reads tens of ohms. If tip to ring reads it instead, move the bias wire to Ring / pin 3.

The rails

  • Trim the servo buck to ~6 V before a servo is plugged in. Check each servo's maximum before going higher.
  • USB unplugged, 12 V on: the USB-C connector's VBUS reads 0 V (the Schottky is doing its job).
  • CT bias node to GND reads ~1.65 V.

The modules

  • OLED: VCC/GND order differs between modules — read yours. It runs from 3V3; at 5 V its pull-ups put 5 V on D4/D5.
  • The MPM3610 breakout's pins follow the LM1117 TO-220 order plus EN — go by the silkscreen.

After it's built

  • Read the CT floor with servos idle and moving: ~6.7 counts (0.195 A) is the number to beat with the new parts fitted.
  • Unplug the clamp: the node should stop reading it (D0 railed high), not read zero.

Drawn 2026-10-08. Not to scale: the XIAO's pads are spaced to fit what hangs off them. The perfboard column is the reference — it works — and nothing in the PCB column or the new CT parts has been on a board yet.
